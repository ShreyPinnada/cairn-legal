Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 September 2026

The short version

  • Your thoughts, photos and voice notes are stored on your phone. By default, nothing you write leaves your device.
  • Cairn has no ads, no analytics, and no third-party trackers, and you don't need an account to use it.
  • On-device AI powers search and connections — your thoughts never leave your device for these features. Cairn+ adds cloud-powered topic tagging (via Google's AI) to surface richer connections; the text of thoughts sent for tagging is not retained by Google.
  • Cloud backup is optional — it saves to your own Google Drive, and only if you turn it on.

This Privacy Policy explains how the Cairn mobile application ("Cairn", "the app", "we", "us") handles your information. By using Cairn, you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

1. Information stored on your device

Cairn is built to be a private, on-device notebook. The following are created and stored locally on your phone and are not transmitted to us:

  • Your thoughts — the text you write.
  • Photos you attach or capture within the app.
  • Voice notes you record.
  • Derived data — mathematical representations ("embeddings") of your thoughts, and the connections between them, all computed on your device to power search and the wall.
  • Your settings and preferences.

We do not have a server that receives, stores, or reads this content. We cannot see your thoughts.

2. What we do not collect

  • We do not use advertising or advertising identifiers.
  • We do not use analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, and we do not build a profile of you.
  • We do not require you to create an account or provide your name, email, or phone number to use the app.
  • We do not sell or share your personal information with anyone.

3. AI — on-device and cloud

On-device models

To power search and connections, Cairn downloads AI model files the first time they are needed. These files are hosted publicly on GitHub Releases (github.com/ShreyPinnada/Recollect-models). This is a one-way download to your phone — none of your thoughts or personal content is sent in the process. As with any web request, the host (GitHub, a service of Microsoft) may automatically log technical information such as your IP address; this is governed by GitHub's own privacy policy.

Cairn+ cloud AI tagging

Cairn+ subscribers receive an additional feature: cloud-powered topic tagging. When enabled, the text of individual thoughts is sent to Google's Vertex AI service to generate short topic tags (e.g. "travel", "ideas", "health"). These tags are returned to your device and stored locally — they are what powers the richer connections Cairn+ surfaces.

  • Only the text content of a thought is sent — photos, voice recordings, and location data are never transmitted for tagging.
  • Google processes the text and returns tags. According to Google's API usage policies, request content is not used to train Google's models and is not retained beyond the time needed to generate a response.
  • We never see or store your thoughts on our own servers — the request goes from your device directly to Google's API.
  • A monthly token cap is enforced on your device, so the volume of text sent is bounded and visible to you in the Cairn+ section of the app.

If you prefer to keep all your thought content on-device, you can contact us to discuss your options. We plan to add an in-app toggle for this in a future update.

4. Optional Google account and cloud backup

Cairn works fully without any sign-in. If you choose to enable cloud backup (a Cairn+ feature), you sign in with your Google account, and:

  • Backups are saved to your own Google Drive. We do not receive a copy and cannot access your Drive.
  • We store, on your device only, the minimum needed to show "signed in as …" and to know a backup destination exists (your account email, display name, and account identifier). We do not store long-lived access tokens; those are issued fresh by Google each time.
  • You can sign out at any time from within the app, which removes this information from your device.

Your use of Google Drive is subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Subscriptions and payments

Cairn+ subscriptions are sold and processed entirely by Google Play. We never see or receive your card number, billing address, or other payment details. Google shares with us only whether your subscription is active, so the app can unlock Cairn+ features. Payment handling is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and the Google Play Terms of Service.

7. Data retention and deletion

Because your content lives on your device, you are in control of it. Deleting a thought removes it from the app. Uninstalling Cairn removes the app's local data from your device. If you enabled cloud backup, any copy in your Google Drive is managed by you and can be deleted from your Drive.

8. Children's privacy

Cairn is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal data. Because Cairn stores your content on your own device and we do not hold it on a server, you can exercise these rights directly within the app and your device. If you have questions, contact us below.

10. Security

Your content is stored within the app's private storage on your device, protected by your device's own security. Cairn also offers an optional app lock (PIN or biometric). No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, but because your data stays on your device by default, its safety is tied primarily to your device's security.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app. Continued use of Cairn after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
