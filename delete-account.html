Delete Your Account & Data

Last updated: 28 August 2026

Cairn does not run its own account system. Your thoughts, photos and voice notes are stored only on your device. The only "account" involved is the optional Google sign-in used for Cairn+ cloud backup — and you are always in control of it.

Delete everything on your device

  1. Open Cairn.
  2. Go to More, and delete individual thoughts, or use Export a copy first if you want to keep a backup file.
  3. To remove all local data at once, uninstall the Cairn app from your phone. This permanently deletes your thoughts, photos and voice notes stored on the device.

Disconnect your Google account (Cairn+ backup)

  1. Open Cairn → More → Account.
  2. Tap Sign out. This removes your account email, name and identifier from your device and stops future backups.

Delete your backup from Google Drive

Cairn's cloud backup is saved to your own Google Drive, in a location only your account can access. To delete it:

  1. Open the Google Drive app or website, signed in with the same account.
  2. Find the Cairn backup file (or the app-specific "Cairn" folder), and delete it.
  3. Empty your Drive trash to remove it permanently.

We do not operate a server that stores your content, so there is nothing on our end to separately delete once you've completed the steps above.
