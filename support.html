Common questions

Where are my thoughts stored?

On your device. Cairn has no server of its own and no account, so we can't see or recover your thoughts. See the Privacy Policy (Android) · Privacy Policy (iPhone & iPad) for the details.

How do I back up?

Cairn+ backs up automatically to your own Google Drive on Android, or your own iCloud on iPhone and iPad — open More → Backup. On either platform, and for free, you can also use More → Export a copy to save an encrypted file you keep yourself.

How do I get my thoughts onto a new phone?

With Cairn+, install Cairn on the new phone, open More → Backup and choose Restore. Restoring merges: anything newer already on the phone is kept. With an exported file, choose Bring back a saved copy under More → Export a copy.

How do I manage or cancel Cairn+?

On iPhone and iPad, Cairn+ is a one-time purchase, so there is nothing to cancel. On Android: Google Play Store → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Cancelling stops renewal; Cairn+ stays on until the end of the paid period. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google.

I bought Cairn+ but it isn't unlocked

Open the Cairn+ screen and tap Restore, making sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account or Google account you bought it with.
